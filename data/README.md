### A2 scenario fixtures

**SYNTHETIC DATA - GENERATED FOR PROTOTYPING.** The texts, tool names (`send_email`, `delete_files`, `run_report`), stipulated risk
scores, `reviewer-sim-01` and `intruder-01` in `src/run_walkthrough.py` (SYNTH_SCENARIOS) and `tests/` are invented by the
developer/AI assistant for prototyping. They are not user data and not real-world observations. Tools are simulated and perform no action.
No real credentials, tokens, e-mail addresses or personal data are present.
